WHAT WE TEACH

Kathak & Tabla

Kathak

Training in technique, rhythm, repertoire, abhinaya, and the artistic traditions of Kathak.

Tabla

Training in rhythm, technique, taal, accompaniment, and musical understanding.

KATHAK TRAINING

Levels

Beginner

Foundations of Kathak including tatkar, Teen Taal, toda, tukda, tihai, and fundamental movement vocabulary.

Intermediate

Developing layakari, exploring different taals, expanding repertoire, and beginning the study of abhinaya.

Advanced

Complex rhythmic structures, advanced compositions, performance practice, abhinaya, and rasa theory.
