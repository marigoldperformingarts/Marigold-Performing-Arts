STUDY WITH MARIGOLD

Training at Every Stage

Beginner

Build foundations in movement, rhythm, technique, tatkar, Teen Taal, toda, tukda, and tihai.

Intermediate

Develop layakari, explore different taals, expand repertoire, and begin abhinaya.

Advanced

Explore complex rhythmic structures, advanced compositions, performance practice, and rasa.
